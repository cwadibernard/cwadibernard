### 🧭 What I do

I take companies off paper, spreadsheets and manual handoffs, and put them onto systems that pass a record to each other instead of to a person. Right now that's as Director of Operations and Technology for a construction industry company in California, where I own everything technical: the ERP, the identity estate, the network, the internal apps, and the AI tooling underneath them.

What I optimize for is change that compounds through other people. I would rather make a small fix that permanently speeds up a team than ship something large that only showcases my own work.

### 🏗️ What I'm building

| Project | What it is | Stack | Repo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Field sales platform** (vertical SaaS) | Offline-first mobile platform for a trade industry that quotes from measurements. On-device 3D measurement replaces manual measuring, a vision module reads dimensions off plans, and a signed quote carries through to job costing and commission reconciliation instead of stopping at the contract. Running in production as standard operating procedure for a full sales team. | React · TypeScript · Supabase · HuggingFace · computer vision | 🔒 Private |
| **Vision AI for officiating** | Real-time multimodal model that classifies rule violations in sports footage, video frames to structured JSON.
